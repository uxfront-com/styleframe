You shouldn't have to open the docs to see what Styleframe does. The homepage now takes you through it one chapter per idea, from a single TypeScript config to the CSS it ships.

### New

- **A rebuilt homepage.** Each chapter holds one idea, like config, themes or the scanner, most with code, an image or a video beside the copy. As you scroll to a chapter's code, particles gather around it.

### Improved

- **Copy for the AI era.** The homepage now leads with a type-safe styling engine for the AI era: the types catch a renamed token before your users do, whether you or an agent made the change.
- **One clear place to start.** Get started is now filled, in the hero and at the end of the page, so it stands out from the links around it. Star on GitHub shows a star in place of its arrow.
- **Quieter headers and cards.** Log in has left the header, on the homepage and in the docs, and you'll find it in the footer of the homepage and the product pages. The feature cards' headings lose their arrows, and the cards are still links.
