Align the Modal recipes with the Open Components Dialog standard. The `useModalRecipe` panel now stays `1rem` inside each edge of the screen and caps its width per size at `24rem`, `32rem` or `48rem`, unless it's `fullscreen`, so you can drop any `max-width` you set yourself. It wraps long titles, keeps its border visible in forced colors mode and at full strength with `prefers-contrast: more`, and stops the page scrolling while a `modal-overlay` is on it, so render the overlay only while the modal is open. In the header, body and footer recipes, the body scrolls while the header and footer keep their height, the footer's actions wrap on a narrow screen, and the padding uses logical properties, so they mirror in right-to-left languages. The drawers share these section recipes and the `soft` surfaces, so `useDrawerHeaderRecipe`, `useDrawerBodyRecipe`, `useDrawerFooterRecipe` and `useDrawerRecipe` inherit the logical padding, the wrapping footer and a `soft` border in the dark theme.
