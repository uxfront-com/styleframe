Align the Modal recipes with the Open Components Dialog standard. `useModalRecipe` now caps the panel at `calc(100% - 2rem)` high and, per size, at `24rem`, `32rem` or `48rem` wide (never more than `100% - 2rem`), so a modal no longer fills the whole overlay and you can drop any `max-width` you set yourself. It wraps long titles, draws its border in `CanvasText` in forced colors mode and in the text color when `prefers-contrast: more` is set, and stops the page scrolling while a `modal-overlay` is on it, so render the overlay only while the modal is open. `useModalHeaderRecipe` and `useModalFooterRecipe` no longer shrink, and `useModalBodyRecipe` scrolls when the content doesn't fit, so the title and actions stay in view. The three section recipes pad with `padding-inline-start` and `padding-inline-end` instead of `padding-left` and `padding-right`, so they mirror in right-to-left languages, and the `soft` panels gain a border in the dark theme. The drawers share the overlay factory, so `useDrawerHeaderRecipe`, `useDrawerBodyRecipe`, `useDrawerFooterRecipe` and the `soft` surfaces of `useDrawerRecipe` inherit the logical padding, the scrolling body and the dark-theme border too.
