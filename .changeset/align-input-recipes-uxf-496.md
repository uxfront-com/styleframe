Bring `useInputRecipe`, `useInputPrefixRecipe` and `useInputSuffixRecipe` in line with the Open Components Input standard. The focus ring is `@color.focus` in every state, and an invalid field shows through its error border and a new 1px inset shadow instead of a red ring. The wrapper now also takes its invalid, read-only and disabled look from the nested input's `aria-invalid="true"`, `readonly` and `disabled` attributes. Hover styles only apply on devices that can hover, each size has a `min-height`, and on touch screens the input's text is at least 16px and its hit area at least 44px tall. Disabled fields turn `GrayText` in forced colors mode, and borders use the text color with `prefers-contrast: more`. The placeholder, prefix and suffix are now the field's text color at 75%, and the prefix and suffix use logical padding. The read-only state no longer tints the fill, and `light` and `dark` ghost fields stay transparent and keep one color in both themes, like every `light` and `dark` field. `useTextareaRecipe` and `useInputDateRecipe` share the field wrapper, so they get the same changes, except that `useInputDateRecipe` keeps its text size and height on touch screens, and `useTextareaPrefixRecipe` and `useTextareaSuffixRecipe` get the prefix and suffix changes. `useOtpCellRecipe` shares only the surface colors and states: its cells get the hover, invalid, disabled and ghost changes, and keep their own `@color.primary` focus ring.
