Bring `useSwitchRecipe` and `useSwitchFieldRecipe` in line with the Open Components Switch standard. Every off track now keeps 3:1 against the page in light and dark mode, with a knob at 3:1 against it: `neutral` is `gray-450` in both modes, `light` is a `gray-200` track with a `gray-500` border and a `gray-600` knob, and `dark` gets a `gray-450` border. The focus ring is `@color.focus`, and the field takes an invalid look from `aria-invalid="true"` (an error border doubled by an inset shadow) and a read-only look from `aria-readonly="true"` (a dashed border). Sizes are in `rem`, the knob is a gradient that moves to the inline end, so it mirrors in right-to-left layouts, and it jumps rather than slides with reduced motion. The wrapper label is at least 24px tall, and 44px on touch screens, so it gives the switch a large enough hit area. In forced colors mode the switch draws itself in system colors, and with `prefers-contrast: more` the off border takes the text color.
