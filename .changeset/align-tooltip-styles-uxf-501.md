Align the tooltip recipes with the Open Components Tooltip standard. `useTooltipRecipe` and `useTooltipArrowRecipe` gain a `placement` axis (`top`, `bottom`, `start` or `end`), which defaults to `top`, the side the arrow pointed from before. On an element with the `popover` attribute, `useTooltipRecipe` now opens the tooltip in the top layer with `position: fixed`, `inset: auto` and the new `@tooltip.offset` variable as its margin, sets `position-area` from `placement` and flips it with `position-try-fallbacks: flip-block, flip-inline`. It fades and grows in as it opens, and only fades with `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. Every other element keeps its own positioning and the `@z-index.tooltip` layer, so tooltips you position yourself look the same. The content now wraps at `32ch` rather than `240px`, breaks long words with `overflow-wrap: anywhere`, and takes `Canvas` and a `CanvasText` border in forced colours mode. `useTooltipArrowRecipe` now draws its triangle in `color`, turns with `placement`, uses logical properties so it mirrors in right-to-left layouts, sits on the edge that faces the trigger inside a top-layer tooltip, and hides in forced colours mode.
