Align the popover recipes with the Open Components Popover standard. `usePopoverRecipe` and `usePopoverArrowRecipe` gain a `placement` axis (`top`, `bottom`, `start` or `end`), which defaults to `top`, the side the arrow pointed from before. On an element with the `popover` attribute, `usePopoverRecipe` now opens the popover in the top layer with `position: fixed`, `inset: auto` and the new `@popover.offset` variable as its margin, sets `position-area` from `placement`, flips it with `position-try-fallbacks`, and registers the `--popover-fit`, `--popover-fit-below` and `--popover-fit-above` `@position-try` rules. It fades in only without a reduced-motion preference. Every other element keeps `position: relative`, so popovers you position yourself look the same. The container is never wider or taller than the screen less `@spacing.xl`, wraps long words, and draws a full-strength edge under `prefers-contrast: more` and a `CanvasText` edge in forced colours mode. `usePopoverBodyRecipe` scrolls when the popover can't grow. `usePopoverArrowRecipe` now draws its triangle in `color`, turns with `placement`, uses logical properties so it mirrors in right-to-left layouts, sits on the edge that faces the anchor inside a top-layer popover, hides in forced colours mode, and sets `@popover.arrow.size` to `0.375rem` (6px at the default text size).
