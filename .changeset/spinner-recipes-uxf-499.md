Align the Spinner recipes with the Open Components Spinner standard. `useSpinnerRecipe`, `useSpinnerCircleRecipe` and `useSpinnerTextRecipe` gain an `inherit` size, which makes the spinner `1em` wide and tall so it matches the text around it, and the spinner no longer shrinks in a flex row. With reduced motion, `useSpinnerCircleRecipe` swaps the turn for a pulse that fades the spinner to 40% and back every 1.6 seconds, and stops the arc from growing and shrinking. The pulse uses `!important` for its duration and iteration count, so it keeps going under the sanitize preset. The spinner has no background of its own, so in `useSpinnerRecipe` `primary` switches to `@color.primary-400` in the dark theme, where a label then reaches 4.5:1 on the page. The `light` and `dark` colors look the same in both themes, `gray-700` for light surfaces and white for dark ones, while `neutral` adapts to the theme. `useSpinnerOverlayRecipe` darkens its backdrop from 50% to 75% black, so a `dark` label on it reaches 4.5:1 too. With the `inherit` size, the spinner also sits `0.125em` below the baseline, so it centres on the text. `useSpinnerCircleRecipe` now uses the `motion-reduce` modifier, so register it first, as `useModifiersPreset` does.
