Make `useButtonRecipe` meet the Open Components Button standard's stylesheet rules. Every label now keeps 4.5:1 contrast at rest, on hover, when active, pressed or expanded, in both themes, so `solid` buttons darken on hover instead of lightening, apart from `warning`, which takes a dark label and still lightens, and the transparent variants use lighter levels in the dark theme. The focus ring uses `color.focus`, the `link` variant is underlined at rest, hover styles only apply on devices that can hover, and the `&:focus` styles that copied the hover background are gone, since the focus ring shows focus. Disabled, pressed, expanded and loading are styled from `aria-disabled`, `aria-pressed`, `aria-expanded` and `data-loading` as well as `:disabled`, with system colors in forced colors mode and full-strength `outline` and `subtle` borders with `prefers-contrast: more`. Each size sets a `min-height` and a matching `min-width`, labels wrap instead of staying on one line, horizontal padding is `padding-inline`, and touch screens get a 44 × 44px hit area. The `light` color is meant for dark surfaces and `dark` for light surfaces, and their `link` hovers no longer turn the text invisible. No color, variant, size or default changes.
